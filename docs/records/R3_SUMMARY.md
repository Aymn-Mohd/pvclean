# E1 Phase R3: matched local sites with real weather

Labels: **MAIN-R3** decides H-R3; **SENSITIVITY** and **DESCRIPTIVE** are pre-registered but do not decide it; **UNREGISTERED DIAGNOSTIC** was added after seeing the results. Pre-registration: `docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md`, "Phase R3", committed alone as ab9dfdb before any R3 run. Generated by `src/make_r3_summary.py`. Money is USD/yr, mean over 50 seeds with the 95% t-interval.

## Summary

- **H-R3: NOT SUPPORTED** (rule applied as written). The planner's mean beats FT in 6 of 12 case-years and is at or below 0 in 6, exactly the rule's limit. 5 intervals lie wholly above 0 and 5 wholly below.
- **The label rests on one small case.** QLD / Brisbane | merchant | 2025 is -6 [-13, +1], with the planner and FT tied on 46 of 50 seeds. If that mean were positive the rule would give PARTIAL. SUPPORTED is out of reach either way, because 5 intervals lie wholly below 0.
- **The pattern is by site.** Seville: the mean is positive in 4 of 4. Brisbane: negative in 4 of 4. Adelaide: positive in 2024 and negative in 2025, for both contracts.
- **What separates wins from losses:** whether cleaning pays at all. The planner cleans more rows than FT in all 12 case-years, and the 6 losses to FT are exactly the 6 case-years where the planner ends below not cleaning at all (descriptive; section 7).
- **R3 changes R1's picture.** R1's Dubai weather has 3.6 full-wash rain days a year and a 29% no-cleaning soiling loss. Here the loss is 2.0-3.7% in Australia and 17.5-17.7% in Seville. The R1 planner-FT sign reverses in 7 of 12 case-years, against the like-for-like R1 series.
- **0.11%/day: NOT SUPPORTED** (SENSITIVITY). Mean positive in 3 of 12, all in Seville.

## 6. Deviations and interpretation choices

1. Choices the pre-registration left open were fixed in "R3 implementation notes" in `docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md`, committed before the run. The ones that matter most: raw-calendar price series (note 1), one climatology per fold that includes the tuning years but not the test year (note 2), the Dubai GHI chain rather than NASA DNI and DHI (note 3).
2. One change to the production chain: Faiman takes hourly WS10M instead of a fixed 3 m/s, because R3 asks for real weather. Section 5 shows the fixed-wind yield; the difference is under 1%.
3. Intervals also reflect the seeded rain-forecast flags, not only dust and row noise as the pre-registration says (note 6).
4. The dust season, storm timing and blackout days stay on the R1 calendar in every site (note 8), as "everything else is unchanged" requires. Section 7 tests this.
5. "No interval below 0" and "at or below 0" follow the R1 convention: interval wholly below 0, and the mean. An exact tie counts as at or below 0.
6. The R3 replay finished in one session, and steps 4 to 6 were completed in a later one. The stored results were not rerun; two cases were reproduced exactly with 5 workers, both before and after the `season_shift` change.
7. After the replay, `src/sim.py` gained an opt-in `season_shift` (off by default) for the section 7 diagnostic. It is not used by any registered result. `src/exact.py` keeps its own season formula; it plays no part in this replay.
8. No R3 blockers.
