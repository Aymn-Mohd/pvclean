## Summary (Phase R1)

- **H-R1: PARTIAL** (MAIN, rule applied as written). Under F2, planner minus FT has a positive mean in 11 of 12 case-years and 8 of 12
  intervals lie wholly above 0. One interval lies wholly below 0: Spain merchant 2024, -74 USD/yr [-129, -20]. That single interval
  rules out SUPPORTED.
- **Small next to the value of cleaning** (DESCRIPTIVE). The planner-FT gaps range from -74 to +420 USD/yr. The planner's own gain
  over no cleaning is 8,100 to 25,900 USD/yr in the same case-years, so the gaps are 0.1-3.7% of it.
- **FT is a weak reference here** (DESCRIPTIVE). FT minus the training-selected best conventional baseline has a positive mean in
  only 6 of 12 case-years (3 intervals wholly below 0). Planner minus best baseline: 10 of 12 positive; 7 intervals wholly above 0
  and 1 wholly below (SA1 merchant A3, -104).
- **Which effect drives each gap depends on the comparator** (ANALYSIS).
  - *Against FT* (both policies are price-aware), the planner wins on **quantity**. It cleans more and delivers more energy
    (quantity +121 to +1,762 USD/yr). Its timing effect (revenue beyond the reference value of that energy) is negative in
    29 of 30 R1 rows across all markets, contracts and folds (the exception: SA1 PPA A2, +10). The generated markets agree:
    merchant planner-FT is quantity +805, timing -180.
  - *Against the conventional best baseline* it is the reverse. The planner cleans less (quantity < 0 in 28 of 30 rows) but
    at better-valued times: timing > 0 with the interval wholly above 0 in **30 of 30** rows (for example, ES merchant 2024:
    +716 ± 57).
  - So the planner's timing advantage is real against price-blind rules, but FT captures it. What remains between planner and
    FT is how much to clean.
- **Forecast dependence** (SENSITIVITY). Under causal climatology (CLIM) the planner loses to FT in 10 of 12 case-years. It
  over-cleans: for example, Spain merchant 2024 has 835 planner row cleanings under CLIM against 526 under F2. A plausible cause,
  not tested further: the prior-year profile overstates the value level after the 2022-23 price drop, and F2 tracks that level
  shift. Under F3 and the oracle F4 the planner's mean beats FT in 12 of 12 case-years.
- **Raw-calendar Australia** (SENSITIVITY). All 8 planner-FT means are positive with intervals wholly above 0.
- **Price audit** (ANALYSIS). Interval-level valuation raises uncleaned-plant merchant revenue in Australia by 1.0-3.4% and
  changes PPA revenue by -4.7% to +5.6%. Spain changes only from 1 Oct 2025, when OMIE moved to 15-min periods.
- **Sanity check** (ANALYSIS). In the single-row linear-loss world the planner's interval is within half a day of
  tau* = sqrt(2c/a) for tau* = 7.3, 15.7 and 41.2 days.
